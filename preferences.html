On-screen buttons

Undo

Folders: start with one and click Add Folder for more, up to 10. Each folder gets its own button on the video and its own shortcut. Removing a folder only hides it; its settings come back if you add it again.
Location: click Choose… to pick a folder, or enter a full path starting with / or ~/. The folder must already exist.
Shortcuts: click a field, then press the key combination you want. Press Esc to cancel, or use Clear to remove the shortcut. Combinations that IINA or macOS already use, such as Command+Q, cannot be recorded.
Changes take effect when you return to the player window.
